How to use

You and the AI take turns. The AI suggests and sketches ideas; you decide what stays and you can learn to draw it yourself.

Quick start

  1. Draw something on the white canvas with the Pen.
  2. Click 1 Ask AI. The AI looks at your drawing and suggests what to add next. The suggestion appears in the instruction box, where you can edit it or write your own idea.
  3. Choose where the AI works under AI turn: Whole canvas, or Marked region, then drag a box on the canvas with Mark region.
  4. Click 2 Get reference image in the AI reasoning panel to see a picture of the idea. Untick Colour for black-and-white line art.
  5. Then either:
    • 3 AI draw: the AI draws it onto the canvas as strokes you can move, erase or undo.
    • Teach me: draw it yourself, one part at a time, by tracing a light blue guide.

Tools

  • Pen P: draw. Set the size and colour under Brush.
  • Eraser E: rub out any part of the drawing.
  • Select V: click a drawing or drag a box around part of it, then drag to move it. Delete removes it, Esc deselects.
  • Mark region R: drag a box where the AI should draw.
  • Background: fill the whole canvas with a colour; White resets it.

Teach me

  • Each step shows one part in blue, with a short instruction. Trace it with the pen.
  • Next moves on; parts you have done turn grey. Prev goes back.
  • Use the Guide slider to fade the guide and Show to hide it and check your work.
  • Finish or Exit tutorial removes the guide. It is never part of your drawing.

Handy

  • ⌘Z / CtrlZ undoes anything, including AI draws and Clear.
  • ⌘S / CtrlS or the download button saves a PNG.
  • Enter in the instruction box gets a reference image; ShiftEnter adds a new line.
  • The Turn log records every turn and who proposed and drew it.
  • Collapse any side panel with its arrow, or drag its left edge to resize it.
